## Booking and paying

### How do I book?
Book by sending us a WhatsApp message. Tap any Book button on this site, tell us the treatment and the length you would like and when suits you, and we reply with free times and confirm your booking in the same chat.

### What if I'm running late?
Message us as soon as you know. The team will do its best for you, though we may need to shorten the treatment so the next guest is not kept waiting.

### Can I just walk in?
We book by appointment. If you are nearby and would like a treatment soon, message us first and we will tell you whether the team is free.

## treatments

## Treatments

### Which treatment should I choose?
Choose by how you want it to feel. Still unsure? Tell us on WhatsApp what you would like from your visit and the team will suggest one.

- Link: See the treatment menu -> /treatments/

### Is a treatment suitable if I'm pregnant or have a health condition?
Please tell us before you book if you are pregnant, or if you have a health condition, an injury or a recent operation, and the team will confirm the right treatment for you. If you are unsure, ask your doctor first. We do not make health claims for any treatment; we can tell you exactly what each one involves.
