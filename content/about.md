## Three things you can count on

### Our menu is our calendar
Everything on our menu is exactly what is on our booking calendar, in the lengths we offer.

### Tell us about your health
If you are pregnant, or you have a health condition, an injury or a recent operation, tell us before you book so the team can confirm the right treatment for you. If you are unsure, ask your doctor first.

### One simple way to book
Everything is booked in one WhatsApp chat: you tell us what you would like, we offer free times and confirm.
